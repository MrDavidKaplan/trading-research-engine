What this experiment establishes

The simulator separates a signal's idealized return from integer-share, cash-limited execution and explicit costs. Previous-bar volume supplies a causal liquidity proxy; it is not an observed opening auction capacity. The test period is untouched by parameter selection in this run. Repeatedly changing settings after seeing this report invalidates that interpretation.

No short selling, options, live routing, order-book queues, corporate-action processing, exchange calendars, dividends, cash interest or borrow fees are modeled. Remaining shares are marked at the last close, not forcibly sold. Input bars must be on a consistent price basis.
